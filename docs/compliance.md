# Compliance rules

`/compliance` (or `npm run consent -- compliance`) checks the records against the rules below. Each rule names its source, what a breach looks like in the data, and the check that finds it. The limits each reading is held to live in the `limits` rows, each tied to the condition that sets it.

This is a record-keeping check, not legal advice. The consent holder stays responsible for its consents. When a rule changes, update this file and the check in `scripts/consent.mjs` together (`/customise`), and say where the new rule comes from.

Sources were read on 2026-11-06. New Zealand rules are the Resource Management Act 1991 and the regulations under it. The government has said it will replace the Act with new planning and environment laws; until a replacement is in force, the Act and the consents granted under it apply. When it changes, the renewal and lapse rules below are the ones to check first.

| Rule | Source | Breach in the data | Check |
|---|---|---|---|
| RMA-EXPIRED | NZ: RMA 1991 s 123 (a water, discharge or coastal permit runs for the term on it); s 9, s 13, s 14, s 15 (the activity needs a consent); s 338 (offences). | An active consent past its expiry date with no renewal lodged: the activity has no consent behind it. | `consent_register` |
| RMA-S124-RENEWAL | NZ: RMA 1991 s 124 (apply for a new consent at least 6 months before expiry and you may keep operating until it is decided; between 3 and 6 months before, only if the council allows it). | An active consent whose renew-by date (expiry less 6 months) has passed with no renewal lodged. Inside 120 days of that date it shows in attention as "renewal due soon". | `consent_register.renew_by` |
| RMA-S125-LAPSE | NZ: RMA 1991 s 125 (a consent lapses 5 years after it is granted if not given effect, unless the consent sets another date or the council extends it). | A consent not given effect whose lapse date is within 180 days. `lapse_on` overrides the 5-year default when the consent sets its own. | `consent_register.lapse_on` |
| CONDITION | NZ: RMA 1991 s 338(1)(a) (contravening a resource consent is an offence); NSW: Protection of the Environment Operations Act 1997 s 64 (contravening a licence condition is an offence). The due rule is the condition's own wording. | A condition past its next due date with no evidence recorded since. | `condition_due` |
| LIMIT | The limit condition on the consent or licence, held in `limits`. | A reading over a maximum or under a minimum with no incident raised against it. `/reading` opens the incident itself; imported history and hand edits are what this catches. A less-than result never counts as over a maximum. | `reading_check` |
| NOTIFY-REGULATOR | The consent's own notification condition (the number of days is `notify_within_days` on the limit's condition; 1 day when the consent says nothing). NSW: Protection of the Environment Operations Act 1997 s 148 (notify a pollution incident causing or threatening material harm immediately; default window 0 days). | An exceedance or spill not notified by its notify-by date, or notified later than the window. A late notification stays on the record. | `incident_register` |
| ABATEMENT | NZ: RMA 1991 s 322 (abatement notices), s 338(1)(c) (contravening one is an offence), s 343C (infringement notices). NSW: POEO Act 1997 Chapter 4 (clean-up, prevention and noise notices). | A notice whose comply-by date has passed with no compliance recorded. Inside 14 days it shows in attention. | `notices` |
| WATER-RECORDS | NZ: Resource Management (Measurement and Reporting of Water Takes) Regulations 2010, as amended in 2020 (permits to take fresh water at 5 litres a second or more: measure the take and provide records electronically each day; phased in by size, the last group of 5 to 10 L/s on 3 September 2026). | A water meter on a take of 5 L/s or more with days in the last 30 that have no daily volume recorded. The 15-minute records stay in the telemetry; this holds the daily total. | `water_take.days_missing_30d` |
| WATER-VERIFY | Same regulations (the measuring device must have been verified within the 5 years ending with each water year). | A water meter with no verification, or the last one more than 5 years ago. Inside 60 days it shows in attention. | `water_take.verify_by` |
| PLAN-REVIEW | The consent's management plan condition. The review interval is yours to set in `review_every_months`. | A management plan with no review on record, or the last review older than its interval. | `plans.last_reviewed_on` |
| PIRMP-TEST | NSW: POEO Act 1997 Part 5.7A (s 153A, every licence holder prepares a pollution incident response management plan) and the Protection of the Environment Operations (General) Regulation 2022 (test the plan at least every 12 months). | An Australian site's incident response plan never tested, or last tested more than 365 days ago. | `plans.last_tested_on` |
| EPL-ANNUAL-RETURN | NSW: the EPA's standard licence condition R1 (supply an Annual Return within 60 days after the end of each reporting period). | An environment protection licence whose condition R1 is past its next due date. | `condition_due` where the number is R1 |

## Notes

- Abatement and infringement fees, consent monitoring charges (RMA s 36) and EPA licence fees are not checked here. Record a fee on the notice with `--amount` if you want it in the history.
- Compliance grades on `/inspection` follow the four grades councils use in the national compliance, monitoring and enforcement guidance: full compliance, low risk non-compliance, moderate non-compliance, significant non-compliance.
- A consent with conditions that run on calendar dates ("by 30 September each year") is set with `--first-due` and `--every=365`.
