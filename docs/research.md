# Research notes

Checked 2026-11-06.

- CS-VUE is an Auckland company founded in 2004 that sells cloud software for resource consents, environmental compliance, risk and management plans. It breaks each consent into its conditions, tracks them and alerts the owner when one falls due, and can take data feeds from environmental sensors. Sources: software directory listings (capterra.co.nz, softwareadvice.com.au, getapp.co.nz) and press coverage.
- No price is published on CS-VUE's own site. Software directories list a starting price per year per feature, with Essential, Enterprise and Advanced Insights plans quoted per client. The vendor site (cs-vue.com) did not load on 2026-11-06 (certificate error); csvue.co.nz is the domain in its listings.
- NZ Transport Agency used CS-VUE to manage consent conditions on the Puhoi to Warkworth motorway, and said that before it, the agency used "a range of spreadsheet-type systems that differed from contract to contract" (Scoop, 2017; InfrastructureNews). Other named clients in listings include Fulton Hogan, Auckland Council and EnviroWaste.
- Water measurement rules were read on legislation.govt.nz (SR 2010/267 and the 2020 amendment, SR 2020/176) and on regional council guidance (Otago, Marlborough, Taranaki) on the same date.
